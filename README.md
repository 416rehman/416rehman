# Rehman Ahmadzai

I build software, then go looking for where its assumptions fail.

- **[DeepZero](https://github.com/416rehman/DeepZero)** — My vulnerability research pipeline. I can define an investigation in YAML, run Ghidra and Semgrep stages, use LLMs to assess results, and resume the run without losing its trail.
- **[Loonext](https://github.com/BytechLabs/Texturion)** at BytechLabs — A shared workspace for business texts and calls. The team can assign conversations and work from one inbox; AI reply suggestions stay editable drafts, and thread summaries quote the messages they came from.
- **[Vectory](https://github.com/416rehman/Vectory)** — Full self-hosted management for Vector fleets: build and validate pipelines, manage devices, stage canary rollouts, schedule changes, and roll back when needed.

I also publish Windows driver research with writeups and proofs of concept, including [ASUS](https://github.com/416rehman/asus-bsitf-0-day-poc), [Lenovo](https://github.com/416rehman/lenovo-lnvmsrio-kernel-lpe-BYOVD-Vulnerability-PoC), and [Razer](https://github.com/416rehman/razer-lycosa-kernel-lpe-BYOVD-Vulnerability-PoC). One of those investigations has a published [CVE-2026-13585 finder credit](https://github.com/CVEProject/cvelistV5/blob/main/cves/2026/13xxx/CVE-2026-13585.json).
